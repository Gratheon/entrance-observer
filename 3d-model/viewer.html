Entrance Observer

A slim 4K camera arch that hangs on the hive entrance. A small opal roof keeps sun and rain off the lens and drips beside the landing board, all cables run inside the right arm, and the compute sled slides out for service or upgrade. The same arch hangs on the beehive scale and the Robotic Beehive.
